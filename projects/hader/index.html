The operating problem

Business conversations do not begin and end with a chatbot response. They become orders, bookings, follow-ups, support cases, staff interventions, contact updates, and records that other systems need to understand.

Hader brings those activities into one operating model while preserving a clear boundary between automated actions and human control.

Technical implementation

Hader separates the interactive application, service APIs, background processing, shared domain definitions, and persistent organization-scoped records. This keeps inbound channel traffic and longer-running operational work from depending on one synchronous request path.

  • Organization boundariesAuthentication, configuration, conversations, catalog activity, orders, bookings, and integrations remain scoped to the relevant organization.
  • Conversation lifecycleInbound events are normalized into durable thread and message state before automation or staff actions change the conversation.
  • Automation and handoffExplicit pause and intervention states prevent automated replies while a person controls the thread and define when automation may resume.
  • Structured operationsConversation decisions connect to validated order, booking, payment-link, contact, and follow-up workflows instead of remaining free-form text.
  • Voice integrationThe telephony bridge resolves the correct organization context, manages the call lifecycle, and routes supported business actions back into Hader.
  • Operational safeguardsFeature gates, consent and provenance controls, automated tests, data migrations, health checks, deployment workflows, and handover documentation support production operation.

Voice as a Hader capability

Voice AI is implemented inside Hader rather than presented as a separate product. The capability connects live telephone calls with Hader’s conversation, order, booking, payment, transfer, and call-record workflows.

The voice capability is deployed and in active client use. It remains a supporting capability within the broader Hader platform rather than a standalone product.
