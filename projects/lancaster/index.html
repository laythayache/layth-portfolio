Lancaster Hospitality Platform

Fourteen official-domain public websites sharing an engineering foundation while preserving the identity, destinations, content, and operating needs of each Lancaster property.

The engagement

Tayseer Laz and I built and deployed the platform while freelancing under Achour Holding. The work led to both of us joining Achour Holding in the same role: Senior AI Systems & Web Engineer | Technical Lead.

We still operate and maintain the Lancaster deployments. The public case study focuses on visible capabilities and responsibility; administrative interfaces, private data, and implementation details are not shown.

What the public platform includes

  • Property-specific experiencesDistinct branding, destinations, rooms, dining, events, offers, and local property information.
  • Multilingual contentLocalized navigation and hospitality content across the languages required by each audience.
  • Booking and inquiriesProperty booking paths, contact workflows, careers, and structured guest inquiries.
  • AI conciergeAn AI-assisted guest information experience enabled across the deployments with property-specific context.
  • Independent property controlEach property has its own content and data environment rather than sharing unrestricted operational access.
  • Search and discoveryProperty metadata, structured information, multilingual discovery, and links across the Lancaster network.

Technical delivery across the fleet

The platform uses a shared engineering foundation with property-specific configuration and content. Shared fixes can move across the fleet, while each hotel keeps its own domain, identity, languages, guest information, inquiry destinations, and operational boundaries.

  • Reusable foundationCommon navigation, room and dining patterns, offers, forms, discovery metadata, and operational behavior are maintained as reusable building blocks.
  • Property variantsPer-site configuration controls branding, routes, languages, content, contact paths, and enabled functionality without treating every hotel as identical.
  • Independent deploymentsEach official domain is deployed and checked separately so one property can launch or change without requiring every site to move at once.
  • Search-ready outputCanonical URLs, structured information, sitemaps, crawl controls, localized metadata, and pre-rendered public entry points support discovery.
  • Guest workflowsBooking paths, inquiries, careers, and email delivery are validated against the relevant property’s public destinations.
  • Fleet operationsHealth checks, automated audits, rollout verification, retry handling, and property-by-property review support ongoing maintenance.

The technical pattern is public at a systems level; the CMS, implementation stack, administrative interfaces, private data model, and infrastructure remain intentionally undisclosed.

Responsibility split

I owned much of the platform architecture, backend and data work, AI integration, deployment system, property rollout, search and AI discoverability, operational analytics, and production reliability work.

Tayseer established much of the initial visual and frontend foundation and contributed substantially to page systems, responsive experience, staff workflows, forms, property presentation, and fleet-wide refinements. We both contributed across application, administration, search, and operational work.
